About MarkMate

The smart student performance companion that helps students and teachers succeed together.

Welcome

MarkMate brings learning data to life—turning marks and attendance into clear insights and next steps. It’s built to support students and empower teachers with timely, actionable guidance.

How MarkMate helps

  • Track marks, attendance, and trends over time.
  • Generate personalized predictions for upcoming exams.
  • Highlight strengths, surface gaps, and recommend focused actions.

Key features

  • Mark prediction using machine learning.
  • Attendance tracking with clear indicators.
  • Teacher feedback and student‑facing suggestions.
  • Progress dashboards with concise charts and cards.
  • Secure data privacy and role‑based access.

For teachers

  • Review each student’s trajectory with easy‑to‑read charts.
  • Share personalized suggestions directly from the dashboard.
  • Track cohort health and spot students needing timely support.

For students

  • See where you stand today and what’s possible next.
  • Understand which habits most impact outcomes.
  • Follow simple, personalized tips to improve step by step.

Design you can trust

A modern, visually engaging interface inspired by contemporary dashboards—clear typography, accessible colors, and intuitive components that make insights obvious.

Privacy and security

Your data stays protected with secure authentication and role‑based permissions. Only the right people see the right information at the right time.

Our mission

MarkMate empowers student success, supports educators with clear insights, and unlocks educational potential—one informed decision at a time.
